## Mechanistic hypothesis

The lead variant 19_6718376_G_C is a missense R102G substitution in *C3* (protein_start 102, polyphen benign/score 0, SIFT tolerated/0.26) that VEP labels MODERATE impact despite benign in silico predictions, and the ESM3 fold of the 1663-aa protein returns mean_plddt 0.761 and pTM 0.566, consistent with a well-folded multidomain protein where a single surface residue change is unlikely to disrupt global topology but can plausibly tune convertase assembly or regulator binding at the thioester/MG-ring interface. The L2G call (score 0.844) is driven principally by locus-architecture features — distanceSentinelFootprintNeighbourhood (SHAP 0.181), vepMaximumNeighbourhood (0.120), distanceSentinelTssNeighbourhood (0.095), vepMaximum (0.095), and e2gMeanNeighbourhood (0.078) — meaning the gene call rests jointly on the coding consequence and on *C3* being the proximal, footprint-anchored target at the sentinel, rather than on colocalized QTL evidence. Differential expression nominates the canonical AMD effector compartments: *C3* is upregulated in retinal pigment epithelial cells (log2FC 1.85, padj 9e-05, MAST) and in choroidal endothelial cells (log2FC 1.51, padj 4.2e-04) in the Orozco 2020 RPE/choroid dataset, placing elevated local C3 production exactly at the RPE–Bruch's membrane–choriocapillaris interface where drusen form. Reactome membership anchors the mechanism in "Alternative complement activation" (R-HSA-173736), "Activation of C3 and C5" (R-HSA-174577), "Initial triggering of complement" (R-HSA-166663), "Regulation of Complement cascade" (R-HSA-977606), and the downstream "Cell recruitment (pro-inflammatory response)" (R-HSA-9664424), consistent with a gain-of-activation allele that biases the alternative-pathway C3 convertase toward sustained C3b deposition and C5a-driven recruitment. The PaperClip review PMC10776198 directly frames complement (and *C3* in particular) as central to AMD pathogenesis, and PMC9301995 ties sub-RPE lipid/Bruch's-membrane deposits — the histological substrate on which C3b accumulates — to the same anatomic lesion, while bio_23d9ccbb77d9 supplies an aging-retina model in which AMD hallmarks emerge spontaneously. The coherent chain is: R102G subtly remodels a surface contact on *C3* → increased alternative-pathway convertase activity and elevated local C3/C3b in RPE and choroidal endothelium → chronic membrane-attack and C5a-mediated leukocyte recruitment at Bruch's membrane → drusen accumulation, RPE injury, and progression to geographic atrophy or choroidal neovascularization, with the caveat that the only DE layer available is bulk-style RPE/choroid pseudobulk and no coloc/eQTL evidence is present in this pack to confirm that the missense allele also raises *C3* transcript.
