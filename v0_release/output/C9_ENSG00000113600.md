## Mechanistic hypothesis

The *C9* lead variant 5_39327786_G_T is an intron_variant (MODIFIER impact, no coding change), so the L2G call (score 0.961) rests on regulatory rather than protein-altering evidence: the top SHAP features are distanceSentinelTssNeighbourhood (value 1.0, SHAP 0.119), pQtlColocClppMaximum (value 0.393, SHAP 0.118), and distanceSentinelFootprintNeighbourhood (value 1.0, SHAP 0.107), implicating a cis-regulatory effect on *C9* abundance that is corroborated by colocalization with a plasma C9 pQTL (the low ESM3 mean_pLDDT 0.455 / pTM 0.320 over 559 residues simply reflects the flexible MAC-perforin architecture and should not be over-interpreted as a structural change). No differential expression rows were returned in this pack, so the cell-type axis is unresolved here and the mechanism is inferred from the pQTL/pathway layer rather than a single-cell signal. Pathway membership places C9 squarely in the Reactome "Terminal pathway of complement" (R-HSA-166665, 8 members), "Regulation of Complement cascade" (R-HSA-977606), and "Complement cascade" (R-HSA-166658), i.e., the C5b-9 membrane attack complex (MAC) that assembles on Bruch's membrane and RPE in AMD. The regulatory variant is therefore predicted to raise circulating/local C9 dosage, biasing terminal complement toward more efficient MAC assembly on the RPE–choroid interface and accelerating the drusen-associated complement injury that defines advanced AMD — a chain consistent with PMC3902040, which directly ties rare *C9* coding variants to advanced AMD risk, and with the broader complement-AMD synthesis in PMC10776198, while PMC11/bio_23d9ccbb77d9 (turquoise killifish) supplies an aging model in which these MAC-driven retinal hallmarks emerge spontaneously.
