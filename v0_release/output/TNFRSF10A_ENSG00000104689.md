## Mechanistic hypothesis

The lead variant 8_23225458_G_T at *TNFRSF10A* is annotated as a splice_region_variant (MODIFIER impact, no coding residue change), so the most parsimonious effect is regulatory — perturbing splicing or local transcript abundance rather than altering the death-receptor protein fold directly (consistent with the ESM3 model's modest mean pLDDT 0.684 and pTM 0.324, which reflect intrinsic disorder in the receptor's cytoplasmic death domain rather than a variant-induced structural change). The L2G score of 0.966 is overwhelmingly driven by a near-perfect pQTL colocalization (pQtlColocClppMaximum 0.947, SHAP 0.115; pQtlColocH4Maximum 0.99998, SHAP 0.070) on top of saturated TSS-proximity neighborhood features (all value=1.0), arguing that the credible-set variant tunes circulating/tissue TNFRSF10A (DR4) protein levels and that *TNFRSF10A* itself — not a neighbor — is the effector gene. The evidence pack returns no differential-expression rows and no Reactome pathway memberships for this gene, so the downstream cell-type and pathway layers are unresolved here and any RPE/photoreceptor localization must be inferred rather than asserted; canonically DR4 mediates TRAIL-induced extrinsic apoptosis, which would plausibly couple altered DR4 dosage to the chronic RPE/photoreceptor loss seen in AMD, but that mechanistic bridge is not supported by an index hit in this pack. The PaperClip literature directly corroborates the locus-to-disease link — PMC13092188 (2026) reports multiple *TNFRSF10A* variants associated with central serous chorioretinopathy and a protective SNP for polypoidal choroidal vasculopathy, while explicitly finding *no* significant association with neovascular AMD, and bio_b16bf94e6c0d nominates AMD causal genes without elevating *TNFRSF10A*, so the human genetic signal here may be more specific to the CSC/PCV end of the pachychoroid spectrum than to classic neovascular AMD. The coherent hypothesis is therefore: the 8_23225458_G_T splice-region allele modulates *TNFRSF10A* transcript processing, shifting steady-state DR4 protein levels (pQTL H4≈1.0) in chorioretinal tissue and biasing TRAIL/death-receptor signaling toward chronic apoptotic pressure on the RPE-choroid interface — a mechanism more consistent with pachychoroid/CSC-spectrum maculopathy than with neovascular AMD, a paradox the pack itself surfaces and that should temper any clean "AMD risk gene" framing.
